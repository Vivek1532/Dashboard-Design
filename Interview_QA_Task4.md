# 🎤 Data Analyst Internship – Task 4
## Interview Questions & Answers

---

## ❓ 1. What are the key elements of a dashboard?

• Clear KPIs  
• Relevant visualizations  
• Filters / Slicers  
• Clean layout  
• Business insights  
• Interactive components  

A good dashboard presents information in a simple, clear, and actionable way.

---

## ❓ 2. What is a KPI?

KPI stands for Key Performance Indicator.

• It measures business performance.
• It helps track progress toward business goals.

Examples:
• Total Sales  
• Profit Margin  
• Revenue Growth  

---

## ❓ 3. What are slicers in Power BI?

Slicers are visual filters that allow users to interact with the dashboard.

• They filter data dynamically.
• They improve user experience.
• They make dashboards interactive.

---

## ❓ 4. What is the difference between Power BI and Tableau?

Power BI:
• Cost-effective
• Easy integration with Microsoft tools
• Beginner-friendly

Tableau:
• Strong advanced visualizations
• Better for complex analytics
• More expensive

---

## ❓ 5. How do you make a dashboard interactive?

• Add slicers
• Use drill-through features
• Enable cross-filtering between visuals
• Add dynamic titles

Interactivity allows users to explore data independently.

---

## ❓ 6. How do you deal with large datasets in dashboards?

• Remove unnecessary columns
• Use proper data modeling
• Optimize DAX measures
• Use aggregation tables
• Avoid heavy visuals

This improves performance and loading speed.

---

## ❓ 7. What chart types are best for trend analysis?

• Line Chart
• Area Chart
• Combo Chart

Line charts are most commonly used for time-series analysis.

---

## ❓ 8. Why is dashboard design important?

• Helps stakeholders make quick decisions
• Converts raw data into meaningful insights
• Improves business strategy planning
• Enhances data visualization clarity
